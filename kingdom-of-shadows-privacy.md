# Kingdom of Shadows: Privacy Policy

**DRAFT. This policy has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer and may change before the game is released.**

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Kingdom of Shadows is developed by Dhanox Studios, an individual developer. Contact: dxstudios0@gmail.com

## 1. Playing as a guest

You can play without an account. Guest progress is stored on your device only. We do not receive it.

## 2. If you create an account

You can sign in with Google, Apple, or Microsoft to use the diamond wallet and cloud save. If you do, we process:

- Your sign-in identifier and the email address provided by your sign-in provider
- Your cloud save (game progress)
- Your diamond and points balances, and a record of changes to them
- Your purchases and refunds, including store transaction identifiers needed to verify them

We use this to run your account, keep your balances accurate, process purchases and refunds, prevent fraud and abuse, and respond to support requests.

## 3. Information stored on your device

The game stores progress and settings on your device. We do not use browser cookies in the game.

## 4. Purchases

Diamond packs are bought through the store for your platform (Apple App Store, Google Play, or Steam). Payments are handled by that store. We do not receive your card details. We receive purchase confirmation details needed to credit your account.
