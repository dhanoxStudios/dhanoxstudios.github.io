# Kingdom of Shadows: Player Support

Last updated: 9 November 2026

**Contact:** dxstudios0@gmail.com

## What to include in your email

- Your device and platform (iPhone, Android, or Windows/Steam)
- The email or sign-in provider you use (Google, Apple, or Microsoft)
- A short description of the problem, and a screenshot if possible
- For purchase problems: the order or receipt number from the store

**Never send your password, recovery codes, or payment card details.**

## Common requests

- **Purchases and refunds:** Refunds for in-app purchases are handled by the store you bought from (Apple, Google Play, or Steam). See our Refund Policy once it is published.
- **Account and data deletion:** Email us from your account email and write "Delete my account".
- **Lost progress:** Tell us your platform and sign-in provider.
